Fluffy Pancakes

Description

Classic homemade pancakes that turn out light, fluffy, and delicious every time.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3.5 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1.25 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Steps

  1. Sift dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until smooth.
  4. Heat a griddle and cook batter until golden brown on both sides.
