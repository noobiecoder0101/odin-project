Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna recipe is packed with seasoned meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 jar marinara sauce

Steps

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles until tender.
  2. Brown the ground beef and mix with marinara sauce.
  3. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 50 minutes.
