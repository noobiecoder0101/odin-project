Easy Chicken Curry

Description

A simple chicken curry packed with garlic, ginger, and warm spices simmered in rich coconut milk.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 can coconut milk

Steps

  1. Sauté onions and garlic in a pan until soft.
  2. Add chicken and cook until browned.
  3. Stir in curry powder, then pour in coconut milk.
  4. Simmer for 15 minutes until sauce thickens.
